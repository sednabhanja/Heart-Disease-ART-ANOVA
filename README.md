# Impact of Clinical Risk Factors on Maximum Heart Rate Using ART ANOVA

## Objective
To investigate the impact of chest pain type, fasting blood sugar (FBS), and ECG findings on Maximum Heart Rate (MaxHR).

## Tools Used
- R
- ART ANOVA
- emmeans
- ARTool

## Statistical Techniques
- Aligned Rank Transformation ANOVA
- Tukey Post Hoc Analysis
- Estimated Marginal Means

## Key Findings
- ST-T abnormalities were associated with lower MaxHR.
- Asymptomatic individuals showed reduced chronotropic competence.
- Elevated fasting blood sugar increased variability in MaxHR.
